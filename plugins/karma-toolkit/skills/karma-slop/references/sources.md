# Research provenance

Original synthesis written for Karma Toolkit, 2026-10-30. No upstream skill, script, theme, dataset, image or complete checklist is vendored. Source instructions were read as research, not inherited wholesale. Recheck upstream licenses before importing their files.

- https://github.com/yetone/kill-ai-slop — observable visual-pattern examples and contextual exceptions.
- https://github.com/funboy322/avoid-ai-design — distinguish code matches, rendered evidence and visual judgment.
- https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable — inspect the rendered whole, prioritize findings and verify corrections.
- https://github.com/Nutlope/hallmark — preserve content/actions, compare before/after and consider what the original did better. Its fixed house-style bans are not adopted.
- https://github.com/uizze/uizze/tree/main/skills/anti-ui-slop — task-oriented product UI, scoped refinement and evidence limits. No hosted service dependency.
- https://github.com/affaan-m/ECC/tree/main/skills/frontend-design-direction — observable screen contract, states and target viewports.
- https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/tree/main/skills/engineering/prototype — optional structural alternatives in the application's context.
- https://github.com/nextlevelbuilder/ui-ux-pro-max-skill — product/platform-aware selection; no automatic generation of a replacement design system.
- https://github.com/addyosmani/agent-skills/tree/main/skills/frontend-ui-engineering — existing product components and reference-led hierarchy.
- https://github.com/heydamir/unslop/tree/4652254daff586ff4739849395a127b56f7f8cc7 — research inspiration from Unslop by Damir Sokolovsky (https://x.com/heydamir), published under CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Independently worded additions connect a short product brief, a post-change character check and static explanatory annotations to the existing workflow. No upstream text, code or visual assets are copied. Fixed aesthetic rules, font menus, mandatory signature details, snapshot tooling and video generation are not adopted; conventional UI need not be distinctive and no extra approval gate or checklist is introduced.

Popularity is discovery evidence, not proof of effectiveness. The local evaluation compares baseline and candidate under matched tasks; its scope and limits are recorded separately from these reusable instructions.
